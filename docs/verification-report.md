## Differences between the repository and your stated rules (not changed; for you to decide)

1. **R2, "UNKNOWN is held as NEEDS_REVIEW":** in the repo, a job with **unknown experience** and no stated years is **filtered** (shown "experience unknown"), not held. Decision D96 does that. Only an **unknown category** is held, per A. To hold unknown experience too, it needs your approval.
2. **R2, multi-batch postings:** the batch reader keeps only the **latest** year in "2025/2026/2027". That's correct for a 2027 graduate. For another graduation year it could be wrong. F3 fixes it with proper multi-batch parsing.
3. **R6, "include undisclosed":** this filter option doesn't exist (see R6 above).
4. **F8:** `ai_usage` and a per-user daily limit, as above.
